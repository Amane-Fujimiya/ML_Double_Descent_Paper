\chapter{Modelling computation and encodings}

Machine learning, and in general, dynamic systems, simulations, mathematical modelling discretization, and so on, are conducted in modern time via the system of computers, or binary-computational systems. Such operations require a treatment in which theories should be used to formalize different notion of the construction and operative nature, for example, \textit{recursive constructivity}, \textit{abstraction}, \textit{ergodic operability}, and so on. Cleaning up definitions about simulation, inferences, running systems, and the mathematical modelling scheme used, is the main focus of this chapter.
